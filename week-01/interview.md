# Interview Transcript

Student: I need to prepare a domain-driven design proposal for a beauty salon management system. Can you help me understand the business domain?

AI Assistant: A beauty salon typically manages customers, appointments, employees, services, and payments. The software should help organize these processes and reduce manual work.

Student: What are the main business processes in a beauty salon?

AI Assistant: The main processes include customer registration, appointment scheduling, assigning employees to services, performing services, processing payments, and generating reports.

Student: What services are usually offered?

AI Assistant: Common services include haircuts, hair coloring, manicure, pedicure, makeup, and facial treatments.

Student: What problems occur without software?

AI Assistant: Salons may experience double bookings, lost customer information, scheduling conflicts, and difficulties tracking payments.

Student: What is the main goal of the system?

AI Assistant: The goal is to centralize salon operations, improve scheduling accuracy, manage customer and employee data, and simplify payment tracking.
